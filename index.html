Our commitment

Respect the sound.

Artists deserve clear permissions, proper credit and transparent terms. Each track must be cleared for the exact uses offered on the platform before it is published.

  • Confirm rights to the recording and composition as applicable.
  • Agree territories and permitted uses.
  • Provide a contact for rights questions or takedowns.
  • Label sponsored placements clearly.

Artist submission interest

Complete this demo form to prepare a text draft on your device. It will not send anything to a server.

Please do not enter passwords, ID numbers or private documents. A public form will require secure processing and a privacy notice.

What we will check before publishing

  • Artist and track information
  • Ownership or written permission
  • Specific streaming and download permissions
  • Countries and duration covered by permission
  • Credit, royalty/payment terms and takedown process

Submitting interest does not guarantee publication. This prototype is not legal advice and does not accept audio uploads.
